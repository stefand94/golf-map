# Pitch draft: Top 100 Golf Courses (GOLF-230)

Status: **DRAFT for the owner**. Nothing has been sent.

## What their trip planner is (BA look, 2026-10-04, top100golfcourses.com/trip-planner)
- An AI wizard in three steps: Where → When → Style. It claims "AI-powered itineraries from 10,000+ ranked courses worldwide".
- Inputs:
  - where you're travelling from;
  - a region or country, or "Surprise me";
  - duration, dates or a season, group size and budget band;
  - course style (links, heathland…);
  - golf intensity (1 round a day up to maximum golf);
  - travel appetite (stay close up to go far);
  - hide members-only clubs;
  - free-text extras.
- **You must sign in before it generates anything**: Google, an email code or a password. In practice it's a sign-up capture for their newsletter and travel offers.
- **The BA couldn't see a generated trip**, because creating an account isn't something the BA does. **Owner: sign up and run two trips (e.g. 7 days in Scotland, links, group of 4; and 5 days in Ireland), and note what comes back.** Look at:
  - Is the output editable afterwards, or a one-shot block of text?
  - Real drive times, or guesses?
  - Hotels? Costs? Per person?
  - A map?
  - Shareable with the group?
  - Can you swap a course or move a day?

  That answer is the pitch. Everything in our "what we add" list below is an assumption until you've checked it.

## The angle
**Their AI writes the first draft; Golftripper is where the group turns it into a real trip.** Don't pitch a replacement. Pitch "your AI's itinerary opens in an editor":
- a day-by-day plan you can drag around;
- real drive times between courses;
- hotels on the map;
- per-person costs across £, € and R;
- one link to send the group.

This keeps visitors on their site longer and gives them a reason to sign up again.

## What they have that we don't (be honest in the call)
- 10,000+ courses worldwide against our ~560 across GB, Ireland and South Africa.
- The rankings, the reviews and a big audience.
- So we're not competing on data. We're offering the planning layer they'd otherwise have to build.

## What we have (check against their output first)
- An editable itinerary: days, order, drag and drop, "move to day N".
- Real driving routes and times, including ferries.
- A hotel layer and "add a stay" on the map; things to see along the route.
- A costs tab: green fees, stays, fuel and extras, per person or total, multi-currency.
- Share links that open read-only on any phone, with no account.
- A map-first phone layout, built and tested.
- Cheap to run: a static site and one small server.

## What we could offer them (from lightest to heaviest)
1. **Embed or licence:** "Open in planner" from their AI result into a Golftripper instance under their brand (white label). Setup fee plus an annual licence.
2. **Revenue share:** their traffic, our planner and affiliate links, and a split of the commission.
3. **Sale:** they buy the product outright.

**For you to decide before the call:** which of the three you'd actually accept, and your walk-away number. The £5k a year target suggests option 1 at about £5k a year, or a one-off sale at a multiple of that.
